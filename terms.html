Terms, billing & cancellations

Effective October 30, 2026

The service

VettedMapLeads provides searchable public business, contractor, certification and facility records, together with separately purchased Business Spotlight placements. Source coverage varies; the service is not a complete directory of every business or industry. See coverage details.

Subscriptions

  • Premium Agency Pass: $79 USD per month for access to the private lead portal, with up to 500 records per connected source.
  • Business Spotlight: $15 USD per month for an eligible business profile. This is separate from Agency Pass access.

Subscriptions recur according to the plan approved at PayPal checkout. Review the amount, billing frequency and payment method there before approving. PayPal determines eligible payment methods. Only the plan you approve should be charged.

Account access

Keep subscription details, sign-in codes and session access private. Access is verified against the subscription’s current PayPal status. When a subscription becomes inactive, access to the associated paid service may stop. Service or source outages can temporarily affect availability.

Cancel or manage payments

Use My subscription for billing information and cancellation guidance. You can also sign in to PayPal, open Settings → Payments → Automatic payments, select the VettedMapLeads subscription and follow PayPal’s cancellation instructions. Check PayPal’s confirmation of the resulting status and future billing. Cancelling a subscription and requesting a refund are separate actions.

Billing problems and refund requests

For an unexpected charge, duplicate payment or refund request, use the support form. Include the subscription reference, charge date and a description of the issue. Do not include full card details or passwords. Requests require review; submitting a request does not itself cancel a subscription or confirm a refund. These terms do not limit rights that apply to your purchase.

Using the records

Verify a prospect’s current information and suitability before outreach. Registry inclusion does not establish a need for marketing services. Use records responsibly and follow applicable source-use, privacy and outreach rules. Do not share subscriber access, misuse the service or attempt unauthorized access.

Business Spotlight

Submit only information and logos you have permission to use. The publication checkbox authorizes public display of the submitted profile. Sponsored placements do not promise a number of views, inquiries, customers or sales.

Support

Questions about the service or these terms can be sent through Contact support.
